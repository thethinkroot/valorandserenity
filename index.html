How It Works

Guided From the First Word

No forms to fumble with, no design decisions to make. Just simple prompts, one at a time.

01

Name

Their name, rank, and years of service.

02

Service Details

Branch, unit, deployments. As much or as little as you know.

03

Photos & Recordings

Photos, video clips, even a voice recording. Add one to start, more anytime.

04

Their Story

In your words or theirs. A letter, a memory, a few lines.

05

Honors

Medals, commendations, and recognitions earned. Add a Bible verse or reading too, if you'd like one.

06

Invite Family

A private link so others can add their own memories.

07

Preview

See the page exactly as visitors will. A theme matched to their branch is already applied, with customizing it always optional.

08

Publish

A quick email confirmation keeps every tribute secure and verified.

Most tributes take 10 to 15 minutes to complete, once you have a few photos and rough dates on hand.
