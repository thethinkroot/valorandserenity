A Real Answer to a Requirement You Already Have

Medicare-certified hospices are required to provide bereavement follow-up for up to thirteen months after a death. Most of that is fulfilled with a phone call schedule or a mailed pamphlet. This is something families would actually want to sit with, offered at no cost to your organization, and no cost to the families you serve.

On Compliance, Stated Plainly

We do not pay hospices, funeral homes, or any healthcare provider for referring families to us, and we never will. There is no per-referral fee, no commission, and no arrangement tied to individual patients. This is intentional, not an oversight, referral payments to a Medicare-certified hospice can raise real Anti-Kickback Statute concerns, and we built this partnership to avoid that question entirely rather than navigate around it.

What we offer instead is either a straightforward goodwill relationship, your organization gets a genuinely good resource to offer, at no cost, full stop, or a flat annual arrangement for a co-branded version offered uniformly to every family you serve, priced as a standard service fee, never tied to referral volume or outcomes.

We are not a law firm, and this is not legal advice. Any organization considering a formal partnership should have its own compliance or legal counsel review the arrangement, we're glad to answer questions plainly along the way.

Two Paths

Hospices

A goodwill referral relationship. No payment in either direction, ever. Your organization gets a real, thoughtful aftercare resource to offer as part of the follow-up you're already required to provide.

Funeral Homes

The same goodwill option is always available, and since funeral homes aren't regulated the same way hospices are, there's more room to discuss a co-branded arrangement if that's of interest. We'll always tell you plainly which conversation we're having.

Why This Exists

What keeps that promise standing is Story Assist, families who choose that extra help fund the free tribute for the next family who can't pay for anything at all. Partnering with us costs your organization nothing and asks nothing in return beyond helping the families you already care for.
