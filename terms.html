6. Story Assist

Story Assist is an optional feature that can ask gentle questions to help someone remember details for a tribute, and can share real, verified historical facts tied to a date you provide, such as who was president in a given year. Story Assist only surfaces independently verifiable public-record information. It does not generate or invent biographical details about the veteran, and it will tell you plainly when it has no verified information for something rather than guessing. Story Assist is currently offered as part of the free tribute-building experience; if a paid tier is introduced, these Terms will be updated before that happens, and pricing will be stated clearly at that time.
