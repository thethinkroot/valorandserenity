A note about Pinning Ceremonies

Has your hospice team talked with you about a Pinning Ceremony? It's a quiet moment many programs offer, a certificate and a flag pin, formally thanking a veteran for their service, often with family present. If it hasn't happened yet, it's worth asking about.
