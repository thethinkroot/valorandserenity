Valor & Serenity Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

Our Commitment

Valor & Serenity was created to help veterans, families, and friends preserve the stories, photographs, and memories that matter.

We recognize that memorial information is different from ordinary website content. It can be deeply personal. Our approach is simple: collect only what we need. Protect what families entrust to us. Never turn someone's memory into a product.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Valor & Serenity collects, why we collect it, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Valor & Serenity, we may collect:

Information you provide

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Your relationship to a veteran
  • Information you provide about a veteran (name, branch, service years, birth and passing dates)
  • Photographs
  • Stories and written memories, or answers given if Story Assist's guided questions are used instead
  • Honors and medals listed
  • Communications you send to Valor & Serenity

You choose what memories you contribute.

Basic technical information

Like most websites, we may receive limited technical information necessary to operate and protect the service, such as IP address, browser and device information, and security and diagnostic information collected automatically by our hosting provider, Cloudflare. We use this information to operate, secure, maintain, and improve Valor & Serenity.

2. Information You Should Not Upload

Please do not upload sensitive personal information that is unnecessary for memorialization. This includes Social Security numbers, VA claim numbers, military identification numbers, driver's license or passport numbers, financial account information, personal passwords, full home addresses of living people, private phone numbers or email addresses without permission, unredacted identity or discharge documents, medical records, or signatures or other information that could reasonably facilitate identity theft.

If we identify sensitive information in memorial content, we may remove, redact, or restrict that content to protect the veteran, family, or another person.

3. How We Use Information

We may use information to create and maintain tributes, display memories according to the visibility choice selected, communicate with the person who created the tribute, protect tributes and the site from fraud or abuse, maintain backups, improve accessibility and usability, and comply with applicable law.

We do not use tribute content in ways unrelated to providing, protecting, or improving the memorial experience without additional permission.

4. What We Do Not Do With Tribute Content

Valor & Serenity does not sell tribute content or personal information to data brokers. We do not sell or license photographs, stories, or family contributions for targeted advertising. We do not sell or license tribute content for the purpose of training third-party artificial intelligence models. If Valor & Serenity introduces an optional feature that uses artificial intelligence to help build, organize, or present a tribute, we explain that when the feature is offered. This is already true of Story Assist today.

5. Your Memories Remain Yours

Valor & Serenity does not claim ownership of photographs, stories, or other original content you contribute. You retain whatever ownership rights you have in your content.

By contributing content, you give Valor & Serenity permission to host, store, reproduce, format, display, back up, and distribute that content only as reasonably necessary to provide the tribute experience you selected. This permission is non-exclusive and royalty-free. If content is deleted, this permission ends for that content except where limited copies must temporarily remain in backups or records we are legally required to retain.

6. Tribute Visibility

When publishing a tribute, you choose who can see it:

  • Just for close family: a private link. It opens only for whoever you share it with, optionally protected further by a privacy word you choose.
  • Family and friends: for your wider circle. This currently works identically to a private link, since a separate invite-list feature isn't built yet.
  • Open for anyone to find: the link works for anyone, no invitation needed. There is no public directory or search feature, so this makes the link openly shareable rather than publicly listed or discoverable.

Please understand that information made available on the internet can potentially be copied or shared by others despite our efforts to prevent misuse.

7. Living People

Tribute photographs and stories may contain information about people who are still living. Please respect their privacy, and do not intentionally publish sensitive personal information about another living person without permission. If you are a living person identified in content on Valor & Serenity and believe it violates your privacy, contact us. We will review reasonable requests to remove or modify that content.

8. Children

Extra care should be taken with photographs and information involving children. Do not submit sensitive information about a minor. Valor & Serenity is not intended for children to independently create tributes where prohibited by applicable law.

9. Service Providers

Valor & Serenity uses Cloudflare for hosting, database storage (D1), file storage (R2), and security. We provide only the information reasonably necessary for these services to operate, and do not authorize any service provider to sell tribute content for their own purposes. This section will be updated to name additional providers (such as a payment processor) as new features go live.

10. Security

We use reasonable safeguards designed to protect information entrusted to Valor & Serenity, including cryptographically random, unguessable tribute links and hashed (not plain-text) privacy words. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the tribute service and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of a tribute or content you contributed by contacting us directly. We will honor these requests personally and promptly.

12. Family and Privacy Concerns

We recognize families may disagree about content in a tribute. Anyone with a genuine concern, a family member, or someone directly depicted in a tribute, can contact us directly. We review these concerns personally and will work with you to resolve them, which may include restricting or removing disputed content while a concern is being looked into.

13. Legal Requests

We may preserve or disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law or valid legal process, or to protect someone's safety or the security of Valor & Serenity and its users. We will not voluntarily provide private tribute content to third parties simply because they request it.

14. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, applicable law may provide rights concerning your personal information, including rights to request access, correction, or deletion. Contact us to make a request; we may need to verify your relationship to the tribute before completing certain requests.

15. Changes to This Policy

We will update this Privacy Policy as Valor & Serenity grows, including once it undergoes formal review by an attorney. We will not quietly change this policy in order to begin selling or commercially exploiting previously submitted family content.
