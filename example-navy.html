Here's a real example of what Story Assist adds to a family's own words. Press a button to see it for yourself.

"He joined the Navy right out of high school, in the fall of 1968. We didn't know much about what was happening in the world back then, just that he wanted to serve."

1968 was one of the most turbulent years of the Vietnam War: the Tet Offensive that January reshaped American opinion of the war, and Martin Luther King Jr. and Robert F. Kennedy were both assassinated within two months of each other that spring. By then, more than half a million Americans were already serving in Vietnam. It was the world they stepped into.
