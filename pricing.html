The One-Year Moment

A basic tribute never expires and is never held back, no matter what a family decides. Around the anniversary, we gently invite each family to choose: keep growing with the Legacy Vault, wind down with a full export, or both. If your veteran is eligible, we'll always make it easy to also keep a safe copy prepared for the VA's Veterans Legacy Memorial, whether you're staying with us or moving on, because your veteran's story should be protected no matter what happens to us. Nothing is ever deleted or withheld to make any of these decisions for a family.

Cost should never be the reason a veteran's story goes untold. If it ever is, reach out and we'll work it out. Help fund Story Assist for a family who reaches out.
