Privacy Policy

The Real Version Is Still Being Written

This page is a stopgap, not the real thing. A formal Privacy Policy, reviewed by an attorney, hasn't been finished yet. Personal data, someone's photos, a family's story, deserves its own real policy, not a generic page borrowed from somewhere else. Until that's ready, here's an honest, plain description of what's actually collected today.

What's Actually Collected

Building a tribute through the guided flow collects:

  • The veteran's name, branch, service years, and birth/passing dates, as typed in by whoever creates the tribute.
  • A photo, if one is added, stored in Cloudflare R2 (cloud file storage), separate from the site's own codebase.
  • The story text a family writes, or the answers given if Story Assist's guided questions are used instead.
  • Any honors or medals listed.
  • The email address used to confirm and publish the tribute.

This is stored in a real database (Cloudflare D1) tied to a private, unguessable link. It is not searchable, not listed anywhere public, and not shared with anyone outside Valor & Serenity unless a family chooses to share the link themselves.

What This Doesn't Cover Yet

The real policy will need to cover things this honest-stopgap version doesn't yet: how long data is kept, exactly how to request deletion, any third-party services involved in payments once those go live, and the fuller legal detail a real Privacy Policy requires. None of that should be assumed from what's written above.
